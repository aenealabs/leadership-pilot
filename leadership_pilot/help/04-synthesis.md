# Synthesis and readout

Across accepted interviews, the AI finds themes; code ranks them.

## Themes and contradictions

The AI groups every accepted pain point into themes and names where stakeholders disagree. It
sees labelled pain points only (`IV-3.2` is interview 3, pain point 2), never the notes or
quotes, and every label it cites is checked against the record. Unknown labels are flagged.

## The ranking

Each problem is scored from breadth (how many stakeholders raised it, not how many times),
worst severity, highest influence, and strategic fit, each weighted. The weights are on
screen and adjustable. Strategic fit is the one judgment: the AI suggests it with a reason,
and a person can change it with a reason (type it next to the score, then confirm).

## Readout and hand-offs

Export the 30-60-90 readout as Markdown or Word, or copy an outline that pastes into
PowerPoint's Outline view. Quotes are included but not attributed by name. Send a problem to
Backlog Pilot's inbox (quotes unattributed) and see its status there, or propose a persona to
its library.
