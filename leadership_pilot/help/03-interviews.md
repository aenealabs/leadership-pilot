# Interviews

An interview becomes part of the record only after a person reviews what the AI extracted.

## Adding an interview

Paste notes, or upload a text file, a Word document, or a Teams meeting transcript (.vtt).
Transcripts lose their timestamps and become one line per turn ("Speaker: what they said"),
so quotes can be checked word for word.

## Reviewing the extraction

The AI extracts pain points (with severity and systems), desired outcomes, and follow-up
questions. Every pain point and outcome quotes the notes, and code checks each quote: found
quotes are highlighted, misquotes and AI inferences are flagged.

- **Reword** a title freely; the AI's extraction is kept with the interview, and the change
  is logged.
- **Change a severity** only with a reason, typed next to it: severity feeds the ranking.
- **Untick** anything you do not want to keep; dropping extracted items needs one reason,
  under **Why you dropped items**.

Then **Accept** into the stakeholder's record. Accepting again replaces the reviewed set.
**Extract all** and **Accept all extracted** process every interview as drafted, logged as
such.
