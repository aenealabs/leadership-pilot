# Audit log

Every action that changes the record, and every refused attempt, is recorded with who did it
and when. Only the tour owner and approvers can open the log.

## Searching

Search by words and filter by person, activity, key (such as `SH-2`, `IV-3`, `WU-4`, or
`RAID-7`), and dates. Categories cover stakeholders, interviews, concerns, status, updates,
and sign-in and access.

## Refused attempts

Recorded as **access denied**: someone without approver rights opening notes, interviews,
the synthesis, updates, or this log, or trying to change them; and an action tried without
signing in. A sign-in that fails is recorded as **sign-in failed**.
