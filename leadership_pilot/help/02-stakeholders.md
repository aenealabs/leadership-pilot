# Stakeholders

The stakeholder map is the shared record the listening tour and the weekly update both use.

## The map

Each stakeholder has a role, business unit, products, influence, and interest. The grid
places them by influence and interest and shows whose interview has been reviewed.

## A stakeholder's record

The stakeholder's page lists their interviews and the pain points, outcomes, and follow-ups
accepted from them (tour owner and approvers only). It also sets which version of the weekly
update they receive (summary or detailed) and on which channel.
