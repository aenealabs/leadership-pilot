Listening tour, week 3. Daniel Okafor, Director of Supplier Quality, Global Supply Chain. 45 minutes.

Daniel opened with numbers: two field escapes this year traced to suppliers building to superseded drawing revisions. "Each escape cost us more than the whole supplier portal project would."

Today a buyer emails a PDF of the changed drawing. "Nobody confirms the supplier received it, read it, or updated their PPAP." Daniel wants change notices sent automatically on release, supplier acknowledgement in a portal, and PPAP resubmission triggered when a change affects form, fit, or function.

Daniel also wants purchasing in the loop: which open purchase orders are affected by a change, so buyers can hold or expedite. "Today purchasing learns about changes from the supplier, which is backwards."

On priorities, Daniel is blunt: "Supplier changes keep losing to engineering-internal projects because the people who feel the pain don't sit in your prioritization meeting."

Supports Marcus's point on export control: suppliers must only receive what they are licensed for. Daniel is not sure who maintains supplier license data today.

Success: no escapes from superseded revisions, and every change acknowledged by the supplier within five business days.
