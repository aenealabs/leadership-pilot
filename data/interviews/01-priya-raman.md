Listening tour, week 2. Priya Raman, Director of Engineering, Electrical Products. 45 minutes.

Biggest frustration is engineering change. "Our ECOs take three weeks and nobody can tell me where they are." Priya tracks escalations in a personal spreadsheet because the PLM workflow view is "unusable for a manager." About a third of ECOs bounce back for missing information, usually the affected-parts list or cost impact. Priya wants the system to stop incomplete changes at submission: "Make the fields mandatory. If it isn't complete, it shouldn't leave the engineer's desk."

On IT: "We submit requests to IT and they disappear. I don't know if something is in the backlog, being built, or dead." Wants a single view of what engineering asked for and where it stands, reviewed quarterly with engineering leadership.

Second concern is duplicate parts. New hires create part numbers rather than search, because search "takes longer than drawing it again." Priya estimates a few hundred duplicate fasteners in the last year.

Plants complain BOM changes arrive in SAP a day or two late; Priya hears about it when a line builds the old revision.

Success in a year: ECO cycle time under 10 days, and "I stop keeping my own spreadsheet."

Follow-up: who owns ECO workflow configuration today?
