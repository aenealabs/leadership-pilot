Listening tour, week 1. Hannah Liu, PLM Platform Lead, Engineering IT (my team). 60 minutes.

Hannah's team runs 3DEXPERIENCE, the legacy CAD vault, and the PLM-to-SAP interface. "We spend sixty percent of our time on break-fix and access requests." The SAP interface is a nightly batch with no alerting; failures are found by plants. Hannah has asked twice for budget to move it to event-driven integration.

The legacy CAD vault from an acquired business unit is the top risk: unsupported software, a failed cybersecurity review, and a vendor contract that ends next year. About 400 engineers still use it daily. "If that vault goes down, a business unit stops."

On requirements: requests arrive by email, Teams, and hallway, "with no way to say no or later." Hannah wants a single intake and a transparent backlog so business leaders can see trade-offs. Hannah pushed back on adding more mandatory ECO fields: "Every field we add, engineers find a way around. We need smarter defaults, not more fields."

Data quality: three part masters after the acquisitions, no common classification, no scorecard.

Team: strong on platform skills, thin on product management. Two people are at burnout risk.

Success: break-fix under 30 percent of time, and the vault retired on schedule.

Follow-up: get the cybersecurity finding report on the vault.
