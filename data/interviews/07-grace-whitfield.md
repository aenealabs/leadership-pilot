Listening tour, week 4. Grace Whitfield, VP Product Engineering, eMobility. 30 minutes.

Grace's business is growing fastest and is the newest to the PLM platform. "We're designing on three systems because of two acquisitions. My engineers lose hours a week finding the right version of a part."

Duplicate parts are a cost issue for Grace: a sample review found 14 percent of new connectors were near-duplicates. "That's inventory, supplier count, and qualification cost we're paying for twice."

Grace's ask of IT is predictability. "Tell me what we will get this year and what we won't. A clear no is better than a maybe that never arrives." Wants a roadmap reviewed with engineering leadership, tied to business outcomes, not a list of tickets.

On the legacy CAD vault: eMobility depends on it for older designs. Grace supports retiring it but "only if migration doesn't stop my teams for weeks." Wants a wave plan agreed with engineering.

On data: no reliable view of data quality across business units. Grace would sponsor a data quality scorecard if it is visible to leadership.

Success: one system of record for eMobility parts, a 50 percent cut in new duplicates, and a roadmap leadership trusts.

Follow-up: confirm eMobility's peak design periods before planning vault migration waves.
