Listening tour, week 3. Elena Kovacs, Manufacturing Engineering Manager, Plant Operations. 40 minutes, on site.

Elena's world starts when engineering releases. "The BOM shows up in SAP the next day if we're lucky, two days if the interface fails overnight." When it fails, nobody on Elena's team is told; they discover it when a work order pulls the old revision. Last quarter that caused a rework event on a switchgear line.

Elena wants same-day BOM availability and, more than speed, to be told when something fails. "I can live with a delay if I know about it. Silent failures are what hurt."

Effectivity is the other gap. Engineering releases changes with effectivity dates that don't match plant schedules, and "we spend Mondays reconciling dates by email."

On ECOs: Elena is an approver on manufacturing-impacting changes and gets "forty notification emails a day." Elena admits approving in batches on Fridays, which adds days to cycle time. Wants one prioritized queue.

Positive: the PLM team responds quickly when called directly. "Hannah's team is great. The process around them is the problem."

Success: zero builds to an old revision, and Monday reconciliation gone.

Follow-up: get the rework event cost from plant finance.
