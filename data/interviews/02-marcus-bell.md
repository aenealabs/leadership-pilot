Listening tour, week 2. Marcus Bell, Chief Engineer, Aerospace Systems. 30 minutes, Teams.

Marcus framed everything around compliance. Aerospace drawings include ITAR and EAR controlled data, and "every time we share with a supplier, someone in my team is doing a manual license check against a spreadsheet." Twice this year a drawing went to a supplier before the license check finished; both were caught, but "that is a reportable event waiting to happen."

Suppliers are the second theme. "Suppliers find out about our drawing changes when the parts don't fit." Marcus wants automatic change notices to suppliers, with acknowledgement, but only for what each supplier is licensed to see.

On PLM itself Marcus is positive: the 3DEXPERIENCE migration two years ago "was painful but the right call." Concerned that any new tooling adds another place to look. "Don't give my engineers a fifth system. Put it where they already work."

Asked about prioritization, Marcus wants a voice: aerospace changes are lower volume but higher consequence, and "a pure volume ranking will always bury us."

Success: zero premature shares of controlled data, and supplier acknowledgement of every change within five days.

Follow-up: get the two premature-share incident reports from Trade Compliance.
