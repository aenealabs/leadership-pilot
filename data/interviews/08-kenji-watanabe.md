Listening tour, week 5. Kenji Watanabe, Senior Design Engineer, Electrical Products. 30 minutes, at the desk.

Kenji showed the ECO screen. "Twenty-three fields, and half of them don't apply to my change." Kenji often guesses values to get through submission, then the change gets rejected anyway for something else. Kenji would rather the system fill in what it already knows, like the affected parts from where-used, than add more required fields.

Part search: "Search finds parts by number, but I don't know the number. I know it's an M6 stainless screw." Kenji creates new parts when search fails, and admits it's probably a duplicate. Wants search by attributes and similar-part suggestions before creating a new number.

On ECO status, Kenji messages approvers directly to find out where changes are. "The workflow knows, but it won't tell me."

Training: Kenji learned 3DEXPERIENCE from colleagues, not training. New hires repeat the same mistakes.

Kenji likes the idea of AI help if it's transparent: "If it suggests something, show me why. Don't just fill a field."

Success: submit an ECO in ten minutes without guessing, and find existing parts first time.

Follow-up: shadow two more engineers to see if the field count issue is universal.
